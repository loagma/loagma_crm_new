3. Copy build/windows/x64/runner/Release/*
4. Paste into installeroutput/Release/
5. Update MyAppVersion in setup.iss
6. Keep AppId unchanged
7. Open setup.iss in Inno Setup
8. Press Ctrl + F9
9. Get installer from windows/installer/output/
10. Share latest setup.exe only
11. Old version will auto-update
